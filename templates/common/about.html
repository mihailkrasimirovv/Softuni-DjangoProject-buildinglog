About BuildLog

A digital journal for following construction works from excavation to handover.

Why it exists

On a building site the same questions come up every day: which works are running late, which contractor is responsible, and which inspection remarks are still not fixed. BuildLog keeps the answers in one place instead of in scattered spreadsheets and notebooks.

How to use it

  1. Register a construction site with its construction permit number.
  2. Add the contractors that work for you, each with its main trade.
  3. Plan work activities by construction stage and assign contractors to them.
  4. Report progress as the works advance and record inspection findings.
  5. Resolve the findings once they are fixed and download a progress report per site.
Rules the journal enforces
  • A permit number and a company EIK can be registered only once and cannot be changed later.
  • An activity cannot start before its site and cannot be moved to another site.
  • Only active contractors can be assigned to activities.
  • A site can be completed only when all of its activities are at 100%.
  • Critical inspection findings must have a deadline.
  • Activities with recorded inspections cannot be deleted.
